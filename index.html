Research & Development

I am passionate about technology and the role it plays in shaping the future of society. I am currently an intern at Microsoft Research, India where I am working with Srinivasan Iyengar on a systems project that intersects with sustainability. Over the last four years at Ashoka, I have explored my research interests in memory systems, trusted execution environments, applications of blockchain, and cyber physical systems. Outside academics, I have worked with the Women in Computing Society pretty much throughout my time at University and I care about building inclusive communities within tech.

I enjoy the iterative process of ideating, designing, and building products. I am currently unavailable for taking up freelance projects but I should be able to early next year.

Over the last few months, I have re-discovered my interest in the maker community. I have been working with Debayan Gupta at Ashoka to set up the university's first makerspace. This spring, I worked on hacking some popular cars currently on Indian roads to test their resilience against known attacks - the results of which I cannot mention here.

I recently started working on setting up a non-profit to help build inclusive tech workspaces by facilitating multidisciplinary experiences for future recruits across the globe. I will link to our work when we are ready to announce.
